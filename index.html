Built for the things that go wrong at a gas plant.

The leaks in a plant's money are rarely big. They're a screenshot here, a few kilos there. Tankline closes each one.

A screenshot isn't money.

Every transfer waits as pending until someone checks the bank and confirms it. Nothing counts as paid until then, and you can see who confirmed it.

Gas in, gas out, what's left.

Tankline takes every kilo sold away from every delivery, so you always know how much gas should be in the tank. If the gauge says otherwise, you know the same day.

No sale disappears quietly.

A cashier can't delete a sale. Cancelling one needs a reason and a manager's approval, and the activity log keeps who did what and when.

Network down, counter still open.

Cash and POS sales keep going without internet, and receipts still print. Everything goes up on its own as soon as the connection is back.

Questions owners ask.

Something else on your mind? Ask on WhatsApp.

What do we need at the counter?

A laptop or desktop at the counter and a small receipt printer. Managers and owners can also use their phones. Tankline runs in the browser, so there's nothing to install.

What happens when the network is bad?

Cash and POS sales keep working offline and receipts still print. The sales go up on their own once the connection returns.

Can my staff still cheat?

Transfers only count once someone confirms them in the bank. Sales can't be deleted, cancelling needs approval, and every change is in the activity log. Each person only sees their own branch.

I only sell gas. Is it still for me?

Yes. The store, credit customers and more than one branch are all optional. You get what your plant uses.

Is my data safe?

Everything is stored online with a full backup every night, so a broken laptop loses nothing. Only people you add can sign in.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your plant: how many branches, and whether you need the store and credit customers. After a short chat you get one clear quote.
